# Tool Survey card

One card per tool. A set of cards is the Tool Survey, the index of every
AI tool you use or might use. Date every fact that a vendor can change.

* **Name:** (the product and the tier you use)
* **Type:** (one of: general assistant, research tool, image or media tool, AI inside an office suite, specialized tool)
* **What it is for:** (the jobs you would give it, in your words)
* **Tier and cost:** (the plan, its price, and the date you checked)
* **Data policy:** (two lines: what it keeps, and whether it trains on your input. Add the date and the page title and URL where you read it)
* **Access:** (how you sign in, and any age rule or district rule that applies)
* **Last tested:** (the task, the date, and the matrix score, in counts)
* **Notes:** (what to watch, what it gets wrong, the type of AI behind its main feature, and when to test it again)
