# Ethics Statement

Write one statement for the whole project, in these five sections, in
this order. Write each section from your prompt log and your files,
not from memory, and name the file or log entry behind each claim.
Two to five sentences per section is enough.

* Project:
* Written by, and date:

## Privacy

What went into a tool, what did not, and why. Name the tool's data
policy and the date you read it.

## Ownership

What you and the organization can claim in the result, and the
licenses involved (the tool's terms of use, any source you quoted).

## Bias

The paired prompt tests you ran, with what you varied and what
changed. Or name the bias risk and say why it was not tested.

## Disclosure

Who was told that AI helped, where, and how. Paste the disclosure
line you used.

## Accountability

Who answers for the result once it is used, by name or by role, and
what that person checks before it is used.
