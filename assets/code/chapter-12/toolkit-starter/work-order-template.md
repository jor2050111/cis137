# Work Order

The brief you hand an AI assistant or agent-style tool for a job with
several steps. You stay the human in the loop: you set the job, the
tool does steps, and you accept or reject the result. Fill every
field before the first run. The five parts of a prompt (Role, Task,
Context, Format, Constraints) sit inside these fields.

* Tool (from your Tool Survey) and date:

## 1. Goal

What the finished job does, and for whom, in one or two sentences.

## 2. Scope (what it may touch, what it may not)

The files, facts, and systems the tool may use, and the ones it may
not use or change.

## 3. Inputs

What you paste or upload, after your privacy check.

## 4. Deliverable and format

What comes back: its type (a runbook, a test plan, a checklist,
configuration notes, a summary), its shape, and its length.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

The must-pass checks, each with the checklist that runs it: the
Output Checklist or the Accuracy Audit.
