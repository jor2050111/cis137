The topic
---------

Tuesday sessions said they got a call that sounded like a family
member or their boss, asking for money in a hurry. I want one short
item that explains what is going on and what to do.

The audience
------------

Neighbors and small-business owners who come to Tech Help Tuesday.
Many are over 60. Some read English as a second language. The
newsletter goes out by email and on paper at the front desk, so plain
words, short sentences, and no jargon.

Length and shape
----------------

150 to 200 words. A short headline. One image with alt text. No
bullet lists longer than three items.

Two facts I insist on
---------------------

  1. The next free Tech Help Tuesday is Tuesday, November 17, 2026,
     from 10 a.m. to noon, in the community room at the neighborhood
     library branch.
  2. Our standing advice, word for word: "If a call asks for money in
     a hurry, hang up and call the person back on a number you already
     know."

One story I would like to use
-----------------------------

Ruth Alvarado comes to every session. Last month she got a call from
someone who sounded like her grandson, Daniel, asking for $1,200 in
gift cards to pay a bail fee. She hung up and called Daniel, who was
at work and fine. After the session Ruth told me we could share the
story in the newsletter, but not her last name or Daniel's name. She
said it to me in person. I do not have it in writing.
