The company
-----------

  F1. Copperwind IT Services is a managed IT services provider in
      Phoenix, Arizona.
  F2. Copperwind has about 30 employees.
  F3. Copperwind serves roughly 40 client companies across the Valley.
  F4. Two support teams: Deskside has 5 technicians, and Network Ops
      has 3 technicians.
  F5. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. Copperwind
      does not offer after-hours or weekend support.
  F6. The service target is a first reply within 30 minutes during
      support hours.
  F7. Deskside closes about 160 tickets a week.


What the teams do
-----------------

  F8.  Deskside handles laptops, accounts and password resets, email
       and shared mailboxes, printers, slow computers, and new-hire
       device setup.
  F9.  Network Ops handles office networks, Wi-Fi, firewalls, and VPN
       connections.
  F10. On a remote VPN ticket, Deskside sends the first reply and
       Network Ops fixes the connection.
  F11. Clients reach Deskside by opening a ticket or by calling the
       Deskside line at 602-555-0149.
  F12. Copperwind does not design websites or write software.
  F13. A nightly backup service for current clients starts Monday,
       January 4, 2027. It has no name yet.
  F14. Copperwind has no client portal.


Rules for anything Copperwind publishes
---------------------------------------

  R1. No prices.
  R2. No guarantees. Never "guaranteed," "100 percent," or any
      promised result.
  R3. No client names.


The Deskside technician opening

  H1. Copperwind is hiring one Deskside technician this quarter. The

  H2. The job is full time, with 8-hour weekday shifts inside support
