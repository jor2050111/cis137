Deskside Technician Notes on the First-Reply Pilot
==================================================

Mei Lin asked each Deskside technician for one short note after the
pilot. She removed the names before sharing them. The notes are in no
particular order.

Note A
------
If this works, does Copperwind still need five of us? Mei says nobody
is being replaced, and I believe her. But nobody has said what my job
looks like a year from now.

Note B
------
If a draft has a wrong step and I miss it, whose mistake is that?
Mine, the tool's, or whoever picked the tool? I want that written
down before we use it every day.

Note C
------
The easy tickets went fast. A password reset reply took me about two
minutes instead of six. The odd tickets took longer than writing from
scratch, because I read the draft, gave up on it, and wrote my own.

Note D
------
One client answered a reply with "Is this a bot?" I said I wrote it
with help from a tool and checked it. I made that up on the spot. Do
we have one answer we all give?

Note E
------
Nobody showed us how to check a draft. I read it the way I read my
own writing, fast. I caught the portal step because I happened to
know we do not have one. A new technician might not.
