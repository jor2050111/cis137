# Memo template

A manager may read only the first sentence, so the recommendation goes
there. Every number names its source, because a reader who doubts one
number will doubt them all. Keep the factor paragraphs in the Use
Evaluation's order.

To:
From:
Date:
Subject:

(Recommendation, in the first sentence: adopt, extend the pilot, or
decline. One more sentence may give the main reason.)

Cost: (the number and its source)

Time saved: (the number, how you reached it, and its source)

Error cost: (what one mistake costs, and its source)

Risk: (what could go wrong, which kind of rule it touches, the
control that keeps it in check, and its source)

Workforce impact: (what changes for the people who do the work now,
and its source)

What we do not know: (the numbers the pilot did not collect, and the
claims you could not check)

Next step: (one action, and who does it)
