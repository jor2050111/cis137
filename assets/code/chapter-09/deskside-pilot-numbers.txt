Deskside First-Reply Pilot: Two-Week Report
===========================================

From: Mei Lin
To: the AI adoption assistant (you)

You asked for the numbers from the first-reply pilot. Here they are,
plain, with no rounding beyond what the team logged. The owner
approves spending, so the memo goes to the owner and to me. The
operations manager signs the AI Use Policy, and nothing here changes
until that policy covers this use.

About Deskside
--------------
* Deskside has 5 technicians. Network Ops has 3.
* Deskside closes about 160 tickets a week.
* Our service target is a first reply within 30 minutes during
  support hours, Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.
* A technician spends about 6 minutes writing a first-reply draft by
  hand.
* The routing rule stands: Deskside sends the first reply on a remote
  VPN ticket, and Network Ops fixes the connection.

What the pilot did
------------------
For two weeks, Deskside technicians used the pilot chat assistant to
draft the first reply on some of their tickets. Before pasting a
ticket, the technician removed the client's name, the requester's
name, and any account details, under the pilot's rules. The
technician read every draft before anything was sent.

Results
-------
* Tickets with an AI-drafted first reply: 40
* Sent with light edits: 31
* Rewritten by the technician: 7
* Caught with an invented step before sending: 2
* Drafting time for a light-edit reply: about 2 minutes

The two caught drafts:
* One VPN reply told the client to uninstall and reinstall the VPN
  app. That is not one of Deskside's first checks, and on a VPN
  ticket Network Ops fixes the connection.
* One password reply told the client to reset their own password
  through a self-service portal. Copperwind does not offer one.

Cost
----
* The vendor's quote for the pilot tool is $30 per user per month.
  (I am not naming the vendor in this report.)

What we did not measure
-----------------------
* How many minutes the 7 rewrites took.
* Whether more first replies met the 30-minute target.
* How clients felt about the replies, beyond what technicians heard.
* How the tool does on tickets that are not password, printer, VPN,
  or mailbox requests. The pilot tickets were mostly those four kinds.

One more thing
--------------
Nobody is being replaced. Copperwind is hiring one more Deskside
technician this quarter either way. Whatever you recommend, the memo
has to say what changes for the technicians.

- Mei
