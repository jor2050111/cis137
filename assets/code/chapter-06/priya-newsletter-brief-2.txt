The topic
---------

Two-step sign-in for email accounts. Some people call it two-factor
or multi-factor sign-in. You sign in with your password, and then you
type a short code from your phone. At our last two Tech Help Tuesday
sessions, people asked what it is and whether it is worth the trouble.
I want one short item that explains it in plain words and invites
people to come set it up with a volunteer.

The audience
------------

Neighbors and small-business owners who come to Tech Help Tuesday.
Many are over 60. Some read English as a second language. The
newsletter goes out by email and on paper at the front desk, so plain
words, short sentences, and no jargon. If you use a technical word,
explain it in the same sentence.

Length and shape
----------------

120 to 160 words, not counting the headline. A short headline. No
bullet list longer than three items. No image this month.

Two facts I insist on
---------------------

  1. The next free Tech Help Tuesday is Tuesday, December 15, 2026,
     from 10 a.m. to noon, in the community room at the neighborhood
     library branch. Bring the phone you use every day.
  2. Our standing promise, word for word: "No one from Copperwind will
     ever ask you for your password or for a sign-in code."

One caveat that must survive
----------------------------

When you turn on two-step sign-in, the account gives you a few backup
codes. Write them down and keep them somewhere safe at home. If you
lose your phone and you do not have those codes, getting back into
your account can be slow and hard. Every version of this item must
say so, even if something else has to be cut to fit.
