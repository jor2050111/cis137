About us, so your notes are accurate: Copperwind IT Services is a
managed IT services provider in Phoenix. About 30 employees. Two

them. Roughly 40 client companies across the Valley. Priya Sharma runs
our community outreach program and has asked whether AI could help
with the program newsletter.

What I need from you:

  1. Sort the inventory below by type: rule-based system, machine
     learning, generative AI, or not AI. One line of reasoning each.
  2. Recommend the two items on the list (or two new uses) that make
     the best first pilot, and say why in plain language.
  3. Draft a short note from me to all staff announcing the pilot.
     Use only the facts in this brief. I will catch anything invented.

Inventory
---------

 A. Ticket routing. Our ticketing system reads the subject line
    against a table we wrote: if it contains "VPN" or "Wi-Fi," the
    ticket goes to Network Ops, otherwise to Deskside.

 B. The camera at our warehouse door. It reads the license plate on
    every delivery truck and logs the arrival time, including plates
    it has never seen before.

 C. The meeting-notes feature in our video-call app. After every call
    it writes a summary and a list of action items.

 D. Autocomplete in the ticketing system's reply box. It finishes
    common phrases as a technician types.

 E. Our password manager. It stores passwords and fills login forms.

 F. The phone system's voicemail transcription. Every voicemail
    arrives as text in the technician's inbox.

 G. A chat assistant that two Deskside technicians use on their own
    accounts to draft client emails. This is unofficial and nobody
    reviewed it.

 H. The server monitoring dashboard. It sends an alert when any
    server's disk passes 90 percent full.
