CIS137 Skills Lab 1A: Everyday Systems to Classify
===================================================

Twelve systems you have probably met this week. For each one, decide
which type of AI it is (rule-based system, machine learning, or
generative AI) or whether it is not AI at all. Some systems could be
argued two ways. Pick one and say why in the worksheet.

 1. A phone that unlocks when it recognizes your face.

 2. A thermostat schedule you set by hand: 68 degrees at 6 a.m., 62
    degrees at 10 p.m., every day.

 3. A streaming service row labeled "Because you watched..." that
    changes as your viewing history changes.

 4. An email filter that moves a message to Junk because the subject
    line contains the words "URGENT WIRE TRANSFER."

 5. The keyboard on your phone suggesting the next word while you
    type.

 6. A tool that writes a 200-word product description when you give
    it three bullet points about the product.

 7. Tax software that applies the standard deduction because your
    itemized total came out lower.

 8. A translation app that turns a photo of a restaurant menu into
    English.

 9. A car's lane-keeping assist, which nudges the wheel when the car
    drifts toward a lane line.

10. A checkers program from 1959 that got better by playing thousands
    of games against itself and remembering which positions led to
    wins.

11. A customer service chat window that only answers from a fixed
    menu of buttons ("Track my order," "Return an item").

12. An image tool that makes a poster from the words "desert sunrise,
    flat illustration style."
