filled card and three blank ones. Copy a card from
`tool-survey-card-template.md` when you need more.

## Card 1: Tool A

This card describes the textbook author's test setup (not a roster
product), the "Tool A" of Chapter 10's fair test. Every line a vendor
can change is left for you to fill, because Tool A is not a tool you
can sign up for. Fill those lines for a roster tool when you reuse this
card.

* **Name:** Tool A, the textbook author's test setup (not a roster product)
* **Type:** general assistant
* **What it is for:** first replies to Deskside tickets from the team's written steps
* **Tier and cost:** (plan, price, and the date you checked)
* **Data policy:** (two lines, with the date and where you read them)
* **Access:** (how you sign in, and any age or district rule)
