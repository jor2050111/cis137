The services page (Weak Prompts 1 and 3)
----------------------------------------

  * Audience: owners and office managers at small businesses in the
    Valley, 5 to 50 staff, with no IT person of their own.
  * Length: 150 to 200 words for the whole page.
  * The Deskside section needs three taglines of 8 words or fewer and
    one blurb of 50 to 70 words.
  * Voice: plain and friendly. Short sentences. No jargon.


The new backup service (Weak Prompt 2)
--------------------------------------

The service does not have a name yet. Do not invent one.

  * It backs up client laptops and office servers every night to an
    encrypted copy stored off site.
  * Once a quarter, Copperwind tests a restore for each client and
    sends a one-page report.
  * It is for current clients only, starting Monday, January 4, 2027.
  * A technician sets it up on site. Setup takes about two hours per
    office.
  * Restoring a full office can take a day or more.
  * A backup does not stop a ransomware attack or a break-in. It helps
    a client recover files afterward.
  * To sign up, a client replies to the email or calls the Deskside
    line at 602-555-0149.
  * Audience: office managers at current clients. Under 150 words,
    with a subject line.


Rules for all Copperwind marketing copy
---------------------------------------

  1. No prices. Pricing for the backup service is not set in writing.
  2. No client names.
  3. No guarantees. Never write "guaranteed," "100 percent," "never
     lose data," or anything that promises a result.
  4. No claims about other companies.
