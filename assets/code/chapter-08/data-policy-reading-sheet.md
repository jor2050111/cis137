# Data Policy Reading Sheet

Fill one sheet per tool. Read the vendor's own pages: its privacy
policy, its data controls or privacy help pages, and its terms of use.
Do not fill a field from memory or from what another AI tool says.
If the pages do not answer a field, write "Not stated" and list the
pages you checked.
