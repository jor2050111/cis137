Industry Application for Artificial Intelligence

Course: **CIS218**
First Term: **2026 Fall**

Final Term: **Current** Lecture **3.0** Credit(s) **3.0** Period(s) **3.0** Load  
 Subject Type: **Occupational**  
 Load Formula: **S - Standard Load**

**Description:** Industry Application for Artificial Intelligence focuses on the design, integration, and deployment of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) solutions. Emphasis is placed on hands-on project work, ethical and responsible AI practices, and problem solving in applied contexts.

**Requisites:** Prerequisites: A grade of C or better in CIS144.

**Course Notes:** CIS218 will require students to create accounts to multiple generative AI tools.

## MCCCD Official Course Competencies

1. Analyze advanced AI systems and their ethical considerations, including bias, privacy, and transparency. (I-VI)
2. Integrate AI agents or applications into interactive, user-responsive applications. (I-VI)
3. Design AI agents or applications to perform multi-step autonomous tasks. (II, III, V, VI)
4. Apply AI analytics tools to interpret complex datasets and generate actionable insights. (III-VI)
5. Create a culminating project that combines AI agents, applications, and analytics to address a real-world problem responsibly. (VI)
