CIS137 and the Foundations of Applied Artificial Intelligence certificate

Official MCCCD context for building Phoenix College's CIS137 textbook and Canvas course. Fetched 2026-10-21.

What this pack holds

Four official MCCCD curriculum pages, rebuilt as readable HTML from the markdown that co-professor/_SYSTEM/tools/fetch_mcccd.py returns. Official text is verbatim, including its own word choices. The raw markdown sits in source/, and build_mcccd_html.py regenerates the four course and program pages after a re-fetch.

  • CIS137 Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals: the course this textbook teaches. Six competencies, five outline sections.
  • CIS144 Applied Artificial Intelligence: the second course of the certificate. Agentic AI and AI-driven analytics.
  • CIS218 Industry Application for Artificial Intelligence: the third course. Integration, deployment, and a culminating project.
  • Foundations of Applied Artificial Intelligence CCL, major 5364N: the nine-credit certificate the three courses form.

The plan

Mr. Vega set the plan on 2026-10-21. Phoenix College builds its own CIS137 first: the textbook in this repo, then the Canvas course from the book. Next year the college plans to offer the whole certificate, CIS137, CIS144, and CIS218, so CIS137 is written as the first course of that sequence while only CIS137 gets built now.

As of the fetch date the certificate page lists Scottsdale Community College as the initiating college and SC as the only program availability. Adding Phoenix College is a curriculum and administration step outside this build. Flag it where it matters. Do not assume it has happened.

The six competencies, verbatim

  1. Evaluate AI-generated responses for accuracy. (I-V)
  2. Differentiate the major types of Artificial Intelligence (AI). (I, III, IV)
  3. Demonstrate effective prompting strategies to improve AI outputs. (II, III, V)
  4. Analyze the current state of AI and its impact on society, including strengths and weaknesses. (III-V)
  5. Apply ethical guidelines to make responsible decisions when using AI tools. (III-V)
  6. Select appropriate AI solutions to address specific problems. (IV, V)

What CIS137 sets up for the next two courses

This section is a reading of the three outlines, not official text. Use it to keep the CIS137 book pointed at its own competencies.

  • Prompting. CIS137 section II (prompt basics, types, effectiveness strategies, iterative refinement) is the base for CIS218 section II.A, Advanced Prompt Engineering.
  • Judging output. CIS137 competency 1, evaluate AI-generated responses for accuracy, returns as CIS144 competency 6 and CIS218 competency 4. The habit starts here.
  • Ethics. Bias, privacy, transparency, and governance appear in all three outlines (CIS137 III, CIS144 III, CIS218 V). CIS137 introduces them. The later courses apply them to agents and analytics.
  • Choosing tools. CIS137 section IV (platform survey, tool comparison, scenario alignment) feeds the agentic tools of CIS144 and the integration and deployment work of CIS218.
  • The project habit. All three courses end with a project, a presentation, and a reflection. CIS137 section V sets the pattern.
  • Out of scope for CIS137. Building agents, analytics pipelines, feature engineering, modeling, and deployment belong to CIS144 and CIS218. The CIS137 book should point ahead to them without teaching them.
  • Accounts. All three courses require accounts on multiple generative AI tools. Which tools is Mr. Vega's decision, and the choice will date faster than the competencies, so keep tool names in callouts that can be refreshed.
