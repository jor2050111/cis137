Description

Applied Artificial Intelligence explores advanced applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) with a focus on agentic systems and AI driven data analytics. Emphasis is placed on building practical skills, critically evaluating AI outputs, and applying ethical principles such as fairness, transparency, and data privacy in the development and use of AI solutions.

Requisites

Prerequisites: A grade of C or better in AIM100 or CIS137.

Course notes

CIS144 will require students to create accounts to multiple generative AI tools.

MCCCD Official Course Competencies

The Roman numerals after each competency point to the outline sections that serve it.

  1. Demonstrate ethical AI practices, including fairness, transparency, and data privacy. (I-IV)
  2. Develop applications that integrate multiple AI solutions. (I-IV)
  3. Analyze key concepts of advanced AI tools such as agentic AI and AI-driven data analytics. (I, II)
  4. Design AI agents to perform applied tasks in various contexts. (I, III, IV)
  5. Apply AI analytics techniques to process, visualize, and model structured datasets. (II-IV)
  6. Evaluate AI outputs for accuracy, limitations, and reliability. (II-IV)
