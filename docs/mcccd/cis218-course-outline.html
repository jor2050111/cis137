Description

Industry Application for Artificial Intelligence focuses on the design, integration, and deployment of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) solutions. Emphasis is placed on hands-on project work, ethical and responsible AI practices, and problem solving in applied contexts.

Requisites

Prerequisites: A grade of C or better in CIS144.

Course notes

CIS218 will require students to create accounts to multiple generative AI tools.

MCCCD Official Course Competencies

The Roman numerals after each competency point to the outline sections that serve it.

  1. Analyze advanced AI systems and their ethical considerations, including bias, privacy, and transparency. (I-VI)
  2. Integrate AI agents or applications into interactive, user-responsive applications. (I-VI)
  3. Design AI agents or applications to perform multi-step autonomous tasks. (II, III, V, VI)
  4. Apply AI analytics tools to interpret complex datasets and generate actionable insights. (III-VI)
  5. Create a culminating project that combines AI agents, applications, and analytics to address a real-world problem responsibly. (VI)
